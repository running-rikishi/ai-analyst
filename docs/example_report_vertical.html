Recommendation: targeted save-motion + early-warning system

Three actions sequenced by impact and effort

1
Stand up a Mid-Market save-motion for at-risk accounts <18 months tenure
142 accounts currently flagged at-risk, with $2.4M combined ARR. If 30% are saved (typical save-rate benchmark), that's ~$720K retention lift this quarter. Action: dedicated CSM ownership + executive sponsor pairing.
2
Wire low-engagement signals into the at-risk score
62% of churned accounts showed declining engagement >3 weeks before churn — current at-risk score weighs only NPS and support tickets, missing the lead indicator. Adding usage decay improves precision without sacrificing recall.
3
Audit onboarding for accounts churning under 6 months
9 of 52 churned accounts (17%) churned within 6 months of signup — implies onboarding gap rather than product fit. Sample these accounts' first-30-day activity and identify the diverge point from successful cohorts.
Sequencing: save-motion first (highest concrete dollar). Engagement signal in parallel (model improvement). Onboarding audit feeds Q3 planning. All three reinforce each other — better signal helps the save-motion target the right accounts.

Glossary

Plain-English definitions for technical terms used in this report

ARR
Annual Recurring Revenue. The value of subscription contracts normalized to a 12-month run rate. A $50K monthly customer = $600K ARR.
NPS
Net Promoter Score. A single-question loyalty metric: "How likely are you to recommend us?" Score ≤ 6 = detractor, 7–8 = passive, 9–10 = promoter. Net score = % promoters − % detractors.
Tenure
How long an account has been an active subscriber, measured from initial signup to the current snapshot (or churn date for churned accounts).
Mid-Market segment
Customer accounts with annual revenue between $10M and $1B (between SMB and Enterprise). In this dataset, Mid-Market accounts have a median ARR of $35K.
At-risk score
A composite measure of churn likelihood combining NPS, support ticket volume, and (recommended) usage decay. Higher score = more likely to churn in the next 90 days.
Save-motion
A coordinated retention play for accounts flagged at-risk: typically pairs a CSM with an executive sponsor and a customized re-engagement plan.
Churn rate
Percentage of active accounts at the start of a period that cancelled by the end of the period. Q1 churn rate = (Q1 churned) / (Q1 starting accounts).
Cohort
A group of accounts that share a defining attribute (signup quarter, segment, plan tier) and are tracked together over time.

Definitions are illustrative for this demo report. In a real report, definitions reflect the analysis's actual data and methodology.
